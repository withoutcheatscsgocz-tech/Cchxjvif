Draw the thief's route. The clock only runs while you draw.

Guards, cameras and lasers move exactly as far as your thief walks. Lift your finger and the whole building freezes. Stop mid-step, look around, plan the next move. Then take it.

Grab the gold, reach the exit, don't get seen.

How it plays

  • Drag to draw. Touch anywhere and drag. The thief walks your line. The line always continues from where it ended, so you can draw from below the thief and keep it in view.
  • Time is distance. Every step the thief takes moves the clock forward. Stop drawing and nothing moves.
  • Waiting is drawing too. Need a guard to walk past? Scribble on the spot and the world moves on around you.
  • Getting seen takes a moment. A cone turns solid red when it is looking at you. Step out of it before the moment is up. Lasers and walking into a guard end the run at once.

What's inside

8 heists, each built around one new idea:

  1. The Lobby: learn to draw.
  2. Night Shift: patrolling guards.
  3. Blind Spots: walls block their view.
  4. Eye in the Sky: sweeping cameras.
  5. Red Lines: blinking lasers, and waiting by scribbling.
  6. The Waiting Game: sentries that turn their heads.
  7. Crossfire: a grid of crossing sightlines.
  8. The Vault: everything at once.
  • Three stars per heist: escape, take the bonus gem, beat the par time. They don't have to come from the same run. 24 in all.
  • Real-time replay. When you escape, the game replays your whole heist at real speed, so you can watch the guards actually move.
  • Synthesized sound. A low drone follows how fast time is moving and goes silent the moment you lift your finger.
  • Works offline. Once it has loaded, the game needs no connection. No ads, no accounts.
  • Android app. The same game as a small APK (about 170 KB) for Android 8.0 and newer. It has no internet permission, keeps the screen on while you plan, and the back button leaves a heist. Get it from the downloads on this page.

Controls

  • Touch or mouse: press anywhere and drag to draw. Let go to freeze time.
  • Scroll down on the title screen to pick a heist. Escape a heist to unlock the next one.
  • Caught? Tap the map or press Retry.
  • Keyboard: R restarts, Esc opens the heist list, Enter goes to the next heist from the results. Click the game first so it gets your keys. In fullscreen, Esc leaves fullscreen.
  • On a laptop, the fullscreen button at the bottom right of the game gives you the whole screen.

Tips

  • Lift your finger whenever you want to think. Nothing moves until you draw again.
  • Start your drag below the thief, so your finger doesn't hide it.
  • Walls block every view. Hug them.
  • Dashed laser beams are off. They brighten just before they switch on, and the ring on the emitter counts down to the next switch.
  • The exit only opens once all the gold is taken.
  • Escape first. Come back for the gem and the par time later.

Saving

Stars are saved in your browser, so this page and the Android app each keep their own. Safari (and every iPhone browser) and private windows may forget them when the browser closes. The Android app keeps them for good.
